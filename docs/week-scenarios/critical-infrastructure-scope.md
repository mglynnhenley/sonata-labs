# UK critical-infrastructure scope

The active suite is now restricted to desks supporting the continuity of UK essential infrastructure services. The [research report](critical-infrastructure-research.md) supplies the evidence, candidate comparison and current source limitations. This supersedes the earlier broad commercial/industrial/government slate.

## Admission rule

A scenario must define **essential service → disruption or dependency → authorised desk action → verified operational or coordination outcome**. It must identify the operator or responsible public body, jurisdiction, supplied rules, counterpart authorities, accessible records and in-world consequence. Membership of a CNI sector is necessary context, not proof that any particular office is a designated critical asset.

The UK focus includes electricity, communications, water, NHS service continuity and government emergency coordination. Begin with England-specific health, water and local-government rules; do not silently apply those rules to Scotland, Wales or Northern Ireland. Government cases qualify through the essential outcome they maintain, not the department name. A local resilience coordination desk is explicitly a supporting role; its inclusion does not assert national asset designation.

The agent coordinates within stated authority. Technical switching, plant control, clinical triage, statutory determinations and emergency command remain with qualified counterparts. This is a scope definition, not a claim that communication work alone proves service restoration. Fixtures must expose receipt, capacity, delivery, continuity and continuing-ownership evidence as appropriate.

## Pilot and publication gates

Start with water reporting and electricity outage support, conditional on practitioner access; the previous healthcare/steel pilot sequence is no longer active. Price model runs before starting them. Do not build or launch a comparison matrix from this recommendation alone.

Each week needs a practitioner walkthrough; source-version and jurisdiction audit; explicit legally sourced versus synthetic rules; feasible event and response clocks; a counted and executed reference route; calibrated outcome checks; complete snapshots and audit provenance; and measured human effort. Claims stop at the observed desk and service representation. A missing technical or clinical model is an evaluation limit, never evidence of prevented real-world harm.
