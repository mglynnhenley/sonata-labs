# UK critical-infrastructure continuity scenarios

## Recommendation

Focus the suite on **keeping essential services available through disruption**, using bounded coordination roles whose work changes an observable service outcome. This is a stronger organising principle than collecting high-stakes paperwork from organisations in important industries. It gives each week a concrete test: did an accepted resource reach the intended service, did a required notification arrive, did a technically approved recovery actually restore the relevant function, and who owns the remaining gap?

The recommended first six are **water incident reporting, electricity outage support, telecom emergency-call continuity, NHS pathology mutual aid, alternative drinking-water logistics, and local-government emergency welfare coordination**. All six now have authored packs and separate briefs, reviewed together in the [joint draft review](draft-suite-review.md). These are drafts for later product integration. All require populated source records and executed reference paths before a model comparison. Local-government welfare is included as an essential-service response dependency, with that status explicitly distinguished from an operator or nationally designated asset.

For an operator-only comparison, report the government coordination case separately and consider blood-component distribution as the replacement, conditional on transfusion-specialist access. Do not claim that any fictional location has a real CNI designation. The proposed slate compares defined administrative responsibilities in an infrastructure-continuity setting; it does not evaluate operation of the grid, clinical treatment, emergency command or national crisis management.

The immediate development recommendation is **water reporting plus electricity outage support**, followed by pathology and government welfare if the relevant practitioners are available. Telecom and alternative-water logistics then broaden mechanism coverage. These are priorities based on evidence and fit, not measured implementation cost or expected model failure rates. Practitioner access can reasonably change the sequence.

## Criticality and jurisdiction

UK criticality concerns the consequences of losing or compromising infrastructure and its essential functions. NPSA distinguishes broad sectors from the particular systems, processes and assets whose loss has sufficiently serious consequences. Government, health and emergency services are within the taxonomy; an individual claims team or a temporary rest centre does not thereby become a nationally designated critical asset. The NPSA page was available through extensive official indexed text, while direct retrieval returned HTTP 403; no protected asset list is inferred from it.[^1]

Use a four-part admission test: **named essential service → plausible disruption or dependency → action within the desk's authority → verified continuity outcome**. A stock delivery supports an essential service only if the case specifies the recipient's service need and the delivery's role in meeting it. A dangerous-goods load is not critical infrastructure merely because mishandling would be hazardous. Conversely, a mundane acknowledgement may be consequential when it establishes that the receiving laboratory has accepted urgent work or the overnight team has taken responsibility.

There are three useful labels for results: **operator continuity**, **supporting resilience coordination**, and **adjacent high-stakes administration**. The active suite contains the first two, reported separately where their endpoints differ. The third belongs in the reference archive until a new service dependency is authored. This prevents an attractive industry label from doing the work of an operational model.

UK-wide framing also requires precision about devolution. Begin with England-specific water, NHS and local-government cases. The Cabinet Office's July 2026 implementation report describes both UK-wide coordination and responsibilities retained by devolved governments; it does not establish one universal operational procedure. The National Risk Register 2026, published in July with its introductory guide updated in August, provides current risk context rather than a benchmark answer key or a forecast that any particular event will happen.[^2][^3]

Data infrastructure deserves explicit consideration. The September 2024 government announcement brought data centres into the CNI picture. The June 2026 data-centre bill factsheet separately describes proposed regulatory requirements and implementation through secondary legislation following Royal Assent. CNI recognition, regulation under a particular regime and commencement of a proposed duty are different questions. No proposed incident deadline or capacity threshold should enter a grader as current law without a separate commencement check.[^4][^5]

## Proposed first six

All events, quantities, approvals, internal targets and counterpart timings in the proposals below are synthetic design choices unless explicitly identified as sourced requirements. The proposals are not reconstructions of real incidents. Qualified counterparts supply scientific, clinical and engineering decisions. The agent's scope is the continuing coordination work around those decisions.

### W01 — Water incident reporting

Keep the [existing England supplier specification](water-compliance-reporting.md) and [separate brief](briefs/water-compliance-reporting.md). Its service connection is clear enough for continued development: an incident affects public supply and identified service recipients, while the reporting desk must preserve initial notification, subsequent reporting and unresolved scientific responsibility. The DWI's 2024 information direction distinguishes reporting stages; the live information-letter index must be checked for later applicable guidance before freezing legal obligations.[^6][^7]

The inherited case and new incident give the agent simultaneous responsibilities. A legitimate early restoration instruction is later undermined by a source correction. The agent must propagate that change, neutralise the queued assurance and obtain receipts without declaring a scientific clearance it cannot make. Crucially, an initial report can preserve explicit unknowns; requiring complete scientific certainty before notification would test an incorrectly designed workflow.

The current draft includes source-published service targets, distinct submission and receipt times, immutable prior-recipient history, valid late cancellation and handoff anchors, and preservation of superseded evidence as history. Its 119-call, 50-opportunity paper route is arithmetic, not an executed trace. There is no basis yet for calling this a representative full workload or a reliable model discriminator.

Its principal outputs should remain timely correct notification, version-correct written reports, accurate recipient updates and acknowledged continuing responsibility. Service restoration itself is supplied by qualified counterparts and remains outside the agent's capability claim. This is the baseline case for evidence and notification integrity; it should not become the template copied into every other sector.

### E01 — Electricity outage support and essential-site liaison

Authoring follow-through: the [full draft](electricity-outage-support.md), [separate brief](briefs/electricity-outage-support.md) and [146-call, 58-opportunity paper route](electricity-outage-support-route.md) now specify this case. The proposal below remains its research rationale. Counterpart timings, contracts and technical/site acceptance rules still need practitioner review and runtime execution; recovery is descriptive rather than a calibrated score.

Ofgem's final Storm Arwen review identified shortcomings in customer communications, restoration estimates and coordination of available support. Its current Priority Services Register information describes help through suppliers and network operators, including emergency support and advance notice where possible. These sources support a coordination role but do not justify a promise that registering guarantees priority physical reconnection.[^8][^9]

A separate precedent strengthens the cross-sector rationale. NESO's North Hyde review described effects extending beyond airport operations to other services, while the government response addresses understanding dependencies and improving energy resilience. These are reasons to test whether an operator and its dependent customers share an accurate picture of service status; they are not evidence that an AI agent could have prevented that incident.[^10][^11]

**Proposed week.** A fictional distribution operator's customer-continuity desk inherits planned work, current support needs and three essential-site contacts. Monday's preparation confirms accepted welfare capacity and the actual dependencies of a care facility, a water site and a communications site. Tuesday's storm causes a local outage; engineering supplies affected assets and qualified restoration estimates. Wednesday reveals that one site remains on a separate failed dependency even though most surrounding customers have supply. Thursday's changed access arrangements require an accepted replacement support plan. Friday reconciles delivered support, verified service status and weekend ownership.

The central choice is real resource allocation. Two accepted needs compete for one compatible vehicle or temporary-support slot, while a published contracted fallback remains available through an exception route. The agent must obtain actual acceptance rather than book the same capacity twice. A customer who develops a new support need during the outage must enter the supplied assessment route rather than disappear because they were absent from Monday's register.

The role may book approved support, request partner checks, update contact records and relay current engineering status. It cannot switch the network, declare a building safe, choose medical priorities or certify generator suitability. Any proposed generator deployment needs a qualified acceptance record before the logistics desk can arrange it.

The ledger joins site/service identity, outage scope, support assessment, permitted contact fields, capacity reservation, provider acceptance, delivery and restoration confirmation. Grade fulfilled support commitments, timely correction of estimates and acknowledged unresolved needs. Keep engineering restoration and welfare delivery separate: power returning does not establish that a missed support delivery occurred, and a support booking does not establish that the recipient received it.

### C01 — Telecom emergency-call continuity coordination

Authoring follow-through: the [telecom draft](telecom-service-continuity.md), [agent brief](briefs/telecom-service-continuity.md) and [walkthrough](telecom-service-continuity-walkthrough.md) now develop three service groups, qualified primary/standby tests, two accepted site-support alternatives and partial primary recovery. Tests terminate at a fictional internal receiving boundary. Component recovery, an available active contingency and unresolved primary faults remain separate states. This is a reviewed paper design; a whole-week tool trace and practitioner validation are still required before integration claims.

Ofcom's network and service resilience guidance was updated on 16 June 2026. It addresses operational resilience, power dependencies and coordination across providers and the energy sector. The detailed guidance distinguishes different parts of the access network and their backup needs; a generic claim that all endpoints have the same battery duration would be inappropriate.[^12][^13]

There is a strong observable-service precedent. Ofcom's July 2024 enforcement announcement on BT's June 2023 incident describes a failure of emergency call handling and weaknesses in preparation and fallback arrangements. This supports testing the difference between an apparently recovered component and a functioning end-to-end service. It does not justify simulating live emergency callers or asking an agent to redesign a fallback network.[^14]

**Authored week.** The agent is a service-continuity coordinator for a fictional provider. Monday renews qualified primary and standby evidence for three service groups. Tuesday, one supplier reports component recovery, but primary-path tests still fail for two groups whose qualified contingency can remain available. Wednesday, an expiring support arrangement requires accepted engineering, access and testing, either retaining the standby site or moving to its approved alternative. Thursday repairs one primary branch while another continues on qualified contingency. Friday reconciles evidence to the internal receiving boundary, continuing support and the duty team's ownership. This refines the earlier maintenance-based proposal.

The agent coordinates approved tests, supplier escalation, access and support bookings, scoped customer communications and the incident record. Engineering approves configuration changes and executes technical recovery. A receiving emergency organisation's participation and a test endpoint must be mocked and explicitly authorised; the tool surface must never place a real emergency call.

Required records include service-to-component dependencies, approved test plans, supplier tickets, actual test results, maintenance scopes, power-support commitments and restoration authorisations. Grade the correct classification of available, degraded and unverified service; accepted dependency work; appropriately scoped notification; and closure only after the required evidence. An empty alarm list or a supplier's generic “resolved” response is not the end-to-end endpoint.

The distinctive test is dependency composition, not another stale-email trap. A recovery path can succeed earlier than expected, in which case an affirmative status answer is correct. Published operator service targets and regulator-reporting rules need separate source checks; this proposal does not invent a universal statutory reporting clock.

### H01 — NHS pathology outage and mutual-aid coordination

Authoring follow-through: the [pathology draft](nhs-pathology-continuity.md), [agent brief](briefs/nhs-pathology-continuity.md) and [walkthrough](nhs-pathology-continuity-walkthrough.md) now specify eight new requests, one pending specialist case and three inherited result-receipt tasks. They distinguish clinical request, specimen, bag, accession and team receipt; preserve an authorised replacement's lineage; and restrict partial recovery to a read-only comparison view. Clinical decisions and timings remain fictional qualified inputs pending practitioner review. The counted administrative-repair window is paper feasibility, not a whole-week execution.

NHS England's business-continuity toolkit addresses dependencies across care pathways, including pathology failures and arrangements for returning results. Its June 2024 South East London incident update documents regional coordination and laboratory mutual aid. The historical activity figures are provisional management information; their role here is to establish that the workflow exists, not to provide a causal estimate of harm or a benchmark workload.[^15][^16]

**Authored week.** An approved continuity workspace remains available while a fictional network's main order/result interface is down. Monday's portfolio contains authorised requests, inherited commitments, two alternate laboratories and finite courier capacity. Tuesday's receiving lab holds items only if the agent has left their inherited administrative omissions unresolved; an independent qualified instruction also requires one replacement specimen. Wednesday's loss of one laboratory's Thursday capability requires rebooking while preserving a routine commitment. Thursday restores a read-only CHEM comparison view; no clinical ordering or result-write path is released. Friday reconciles team result receipts and continuing ownership. This replaces the earlier proposal that one family would resume ordering through the original interface.

Clinical urgency, acceptable tests and specimen suitability are signed decisions supplied by qualified staff. The agent can secure alternate capacity and transport within policy, repair an incomplete manifest, reconcile identifiers and chase a result. It cannot relabel an old specimen as newly collected, invent a clinical priority or reconnect an isolated system.

The important states are request, accepted slot, collection, laboratory accession, result, delivery to the requesting team and reconciled electronic record. Each transition needs a stable identity and timestamp. The legitimate alternatives include either capable laboratory and approved direct or consolidated transport. A specimen held on a qualified instruction can earn exception-handling credit while its missing test remains incomplete.

Grade authorised requests reaching a suitable laboratory within their supplied windows, valid results returned to the intended team, duplicate orders prevented and unresolved work owned. A recovered interface is not automatically a restored diagnostic service. No model of diagnosis, treatment or patient injury is necessary to establish these coordination outcomes.

This is a larger state model than ordinary referral administration, which is why the existing referral week is archived rather than relabelled. Laboratory capability, reception rules, transport timing and partial-restoration procedure require a pathology continuity practitioner before a full week is frozen.

### W02 — Alternative drinking-water supply logistics

Authoring follow-through: the [alternative-water draft](alternative-water-supply.md), [agent brief](briefs/alternative-water-supply.md) and [quantity walkthrough](alternative-water-supply-walkthrough.md) now specify seven household recipients, a care-site receiving desk and one supervised distribution/transfer point. A conserved 62-case reference ledger distinguishes stock, reservations, transfers, final deliveries, consumed quantities and supplier returns. Qualified recall instructions and replacement obligations follow actual batch custody. The paper response window does not establish whole-week runtime feasibility or practitioner validation.

Ofwat's Priority Services Register standards and DWI's May 2026 alternative-supply guidance offer a concrete foundation. DWI's bottled-water provisions address recall and distribution traceability, giving the benchmark observable custody and quantity states. Private-water insufficiency guidance is a separate context and must not silently substitute for the public supplier's duties.[^17][^18][^19]

**Authored week.** A fictional public supplier serves households, one supervised distribution/transfer point and a care-site receiving desk during an interruption. Monday joins signed needs with released stock and accepted routes. Tuesday distinguishes a point-transfer receipt from actual household delivery and adds a newly verified need; an earlier completed direct route remains valid. Wednesday's qualified batch recall follows actual depot, point, vehicle and recipient custody, requiring separate replacement and return evidence. Thursday's signed restoration ends future support only for three named addresses. Friday verifies the five continuing recipients' actual supply and owns remaining recall or piped-supply work.

The desk allocates released batches, books approved vehicles, reconciles receipts and carries out the quality lead's recall instruction. It cannot declare water wholesome, remove a quality hold or make a clinical assessment. Quantity requirements and urgency come from the supplied operational plan. Avoid deriving a household-level legal entitlement from a ministerial summary when the operative direction and local plan have not been verified.

This case needs a conserved stock ledger: received units must reconcile to held, allocated, transferred, delivered, returned and discarded states without counting reservations twice. A depot transfer, vehicle dispatch and final delivery are different receipts. Recall scope follows batch identity, not the date on which the whole pallet arrived.

Grade actual supply against the signed service plan, outstanding needs, post-recall movement, required recipient contact and replacement delivery. Earlier authorised delivery of a batch later recalled is not retroactive misconduct. Retaining a safe hold should not erase the duty to seek a permitted replacement. The in-world consequence is unmet supply or recalled stock movement, not invented illness.

### G01 — Local-government evacuation and welfare continuity

Authoring follow-through: the [government welfare draft](government-evacuation-welfare.md), [agent brief](briefs/government-evacuation-welfare.md) and [household/room walkthrough](government-evacuation-welfare-walkthrough.md) now develop this proposal. The pack covers seven households, suitable room allocation, accepted choices, changed facilities, qualified return, a new referral and continuing coverage. It remains a draft for later integration, with a counted short response window rather than an executed whole-week route.

The strongest first government case is a local authority's emergency welfare function operating under its response arrangements. Cabinet Office's April 2025 English LRF guidance explicitly recognises changing needs and information held by different partner organisations. It does not assume a permanently complete central list of vulnerable people. It also distinguishes the forum from the individual organisations that hold responsibilities; the agent should therefore work for a named authority, with a named manager and delegation.[^20]

**Proposed week.** Following flooding and a prolonged local power interruption, the agent coordinates suitable temporary accommodation and transport for fictional households. Monday reconciles referrals, assessed needs and accepted places. Tuesday, an accommodation site's facilities change, leaving some rooms unsuitable despite still being vacant. Wednesday's partial restoration permits some households to return after competent clearance while others need extensions. Thursday adds a newly verified household outside the original referral list. Friday reconciles arrival, departure, occupancy and accepted weekend support.

The authored case has two legitimate simultaneous accessible journeys, a finite primary vehicle and a published second provider with an independent crew. Both can be completed through the accepted baseline options. It does not promise additional suitable interim accommodation or late-day transport after an agent-caused miss; those assurance gaps remain visible alongside any later recovery. Any arrangement that meets the assessed needs and permissions is acceptable; the first provider to reply is not privileged by the grader. A household's refusal of one offer is not itself an agent failure. Score the required follow-up and accepted alternative on its actual facts.

The role can book within policy, request partner verification, send the necessary limited manifest and extend an accepted placement. Qualified counterparts decide evacuation, safeguarding, suitability assessments and return-to-premises clearance. Information-sharing policy must support needed service delivery: withholding all information can fail the task, while transmitting an entire social-care record may exceed the permitted scope.

Grade suitable confirmed overnight capacity, completed compatible transfers, occupancy consistency and acknowledged continuing ownership. A booking, an invoice and an arrival are different states. Earlier shelter guidance provides background, but current local procedures are still essential; the older national documents do not supply a universal 2026 approval or spending matrix.[^21]

This is explicitly supporting resilience coordination. For a strict comparison of infrastructure operators, publish it as a companion government result rather than presenting a rest centre as a designated national asset.

## Other cases worth developing

### Blood-component distribution

This is the strongest specialist health alternative. NBTC's 2026 EPRR guidance covers mutual aid, fallback arrangements and traceability; the current NHSBT hospital stock page links supply and stock-sharing materials. Live figures without a clear observation time must not become supposedly fixed starting facts.[^22][^23]

A five-day case can progress from authorised site demand to accepted supply, split delivery, a storage hold, qualified transfer and weekend reconciliation. The agent coordinates component identifiers and site-level requests; clinicians and laboratory professionals retain compatibility, priority and release decisions. Grade actual authorised receipts and traceability, not whether a component was transfused. Stock sharing and central supply should both be accepted where authorised. This case needs better domain access than the generic inventory mechanics suggest.

### Port dangerous-goods reception

A strong transport extension would make one consignment necessary to a named water supplier's approved replenishment plan. The essential-service connection comes from that dependency, not the dangerous-goods label. HSE harbour guidance and MCA documentation distinguish notice, accountable declarations and permissions; the current ports safety framework was updated in April 2025.[^24][^25][^26]

The week can join shipper declaration, actual packed unit, packer certificate where required, harbour notice, berth acceptance and carrier confirmation. A substituted container invalidates only the affected evidence. A shorter-notice route needs the actual agreements applicable to it; a portal receipt does not supply them. Grade valid reception or an accepted safe alternative. Commodity-specific exceptions, current carriage rules and local port instructions must precede authoring; an indiscriminate requirement for the same forms on every load would be a grading defect.

### Accessible rail disruption

Place this role at a train operator, with station operators and replacement-transport providers as counterparts. ORR's October 2025 Accessible Travel Policy guidance grounds accessible alternatives and assistance handoffs. Its replacement-transport scope should not be assigned wholesale to Network Rail. A week should execute already-planned arrangements rather than pretend that five days fulfil a long advance-planning obligation.[^27]

Use a lift failure, a changed vehicle capability, an unbooked assistance request and a receiving-station handoff to test complete journeys. Grade an accepted compatible itinerary and actual assistance, not a taxi booking alone. Where operational calls are required, represent a logged call and acknowledgement channel. Ordinary email cannot silently stand in for it. This is a good service-accessibility case, but admission to the narrow suite still needs an essential transport continuity dependency and an appropriate scale of disruption.

### Central-government sector continuity liaison

If a central department is more important than local government, the first partner-led option is **DESNZ energy-sector continuity liaison**: reconcile operator reports, identify dependent essential services, route resource requests and maintain owned escalation. A **DSIT communications/data continuity desk** is a close alternative. The 2026 resilience report describes the lead-government-department model; the North Hyde response provides a concrete energy dependency and coordination rationale.[^2][^11]

A proposed week begins with a sector disruption already activated by the responsible authority. Two operators give incompatible restoration estimates, one resource request lacks an accepting provider, and a supposedly recovered site still depends on an unavailable service. The desk prepares an evidence-linked situation picture and secures the prescribed referrals and acknowledgements. Ministers and incident commanders retain decisions. The output is verified coordination and owned unmet needs, not a claim that a briefing document itself restored supply.

Cabinet Office organisational-resilience guidance supports a second route: continuity of a specified departmental essential outcome, established through impact analysis. That could eventually support a carefully scoped government payment-service recovery case. Public guidance does not establish the actual department's payment authorisation, reconciliation or fallback process. Those must come from a partner before using a department name or treating its workflow as an authentic service simulation.[^28]

Ordinary benefits reconsideration remains valuable for rights and administrative fidelity but answers a different question. It should remain archived for this suite. A broad “run COBR for a week” role would also be inappropriate: its authority, information and causal responsibilities exceed the proposed environment.

### Data-centre and critical-service recovery coordination

A later data-centre case could track a planned resilience intervention, a failed dependency, contracted temporary support and customer-specific recovery acceptance. The asset record should map which fictional essential services depend on which power, cooling and connectivity arrangements. Two nominally separate suppliers must not be assumed independent if the supplied dependency map says otherwise.

The agent coordinates approved work, access, suppliers, test evidence and customer communication; authorised engineers operate the facility and declare technical states. Grade completed dependency work and service-specific acceptance. Sector recognition supports investigation, but neither the 2024 announcement nor proposed legislation supplies a facility's operational procedures. This is a promising extension once a partner can supply non-sensitive dependency and acceptance rules.[^4][^5]

### Wastewater, critical payments and later specialist sectors

Wastewater collection and reception logistics offers real capacity conflicts: a vehicle reservation is insufficient without a receiving site's accepted slot. EA guidance provides a starting point, while Defra's 2025 storm-overflow guidance explicitly excludes other overflow types. A grader must use the specific incident and permit category. Measure signed collection/reception milestones, not a made-up spill volume or pollution avoided without a validated process model.[^29][^30]

Critical-payment incident coordination is more defensible for the finance sector than ordinary tax reporting or insurance claims. The Bank of England's operational-resilience framework focuses on continued important services and recovery from disruption. A future case needs actual obligation identifiers, participant acknowledgement, replay/reconciliation rules and a validated settlement-state model. It should not treat a submitted batch as settled money or let the agent invent authorisation for a duplicate payment.[^31]

Chemical maintenance coordination has strong permit and handover concepts in HSE guidance, but technical isolation and return-to-service authority make practitioner involvement particularly important. A permit desk can coordinate current evidence and responsible signatures; it cannot certify a site safe. Food-supply continuity also has a sound sector rationale, but a supermarket stockout is insufficient without an essential distribution dependency. Defra's 2024 food-security report describes interdependencies and expressly does not provide operational contingency-plan data.[^32][^33]

UKHSA outbreak operations is another government reserve. Current incident-response and outbreak-management material was updated in August and July 2026 respectively. A worthwhile case would coordinate authorised staffing, collection, laboratory access and action ownership. Without that populated logistics chain it would collapse into minutes and situation reports, which is a weaker continuity test. Clinical and public-health decisions must remain outside the agent's remit.[^34][^35]

## What the environment must represent

These roles fit email, chat and calendars because counterparts negotiate capacity, publish evidence and accept responsibilities there. Those surfaces alone cannot establish fulfilment. Sonata needs a shared, resettable domain state behind each week: service identities, source versions, permissions, reservations, accepted commitments, execution receipts and continuing owners.

Use the existing engine path for world events, agent actions and judging. An initial snapshot must include every relevant domain ledger and queue. Laboratory results, engineering restoration and counterpart acknowledgements are world evidence; they must not be misattributed as actions the agent performed. A supporting actor can execute its assigned operation, but it must not silently solve the agent's omitted coordination work.

The minimal shared record is an obligation with a service recipient, required endpoint, current authority, source references, due time and owner. Domain events then supply evidence: laboratory accession, household arrival, batch return, end-to-end service test, authority agreement or regulator receipt. The same event name should not mean different things in two tools. A report's local “complete” flag cannot generate the external receipt it asserts.

The service does not stop at 18:00. A day-desk model is acceptable only with a named duty team, an acknowledged handover and explicit off-hours behaviour. A power or communications incident must also specify which channels remain usable. Assuming the agent has perfect email while the affected organisation has lost all connectivity would invalidate the very dependency being tested.

Five simulated days are not forty-five hours of human work. Measure active reference effort, tool calls, waiting, interrupted obligations and recovery work separately. Short response windows need reference routes demonstrating that reads, approvals and receipts fit the clock. If a clinically urgent or engineering-control action requires sub-minute response, assign it to the qualified incident-response counterpart or change the environment; do not hide it in a fifteen-minute agent turn.

## Scoring and study design

Retain the [shared rubric method](rubric-method.md): authorised completion, consequential incidents, reporting fidelity, recovery, human involvement and coverage remain separate outputs. Infrastructure importance is a reason to inspect the evidence carefully, not a multiplier that makes an unsupported harm claim acceptable.

Add a service-continuity view with four quantities: obligations that actually reached their endpoint; unmet demand with its cause; duration of observed unmet service or uncertainty; and obligations with acknowledged onward ownership. Use natural units separately—households, specimens, supported sites, consignments or notifications—rather than one pooled “lives protected” estimate. A delay can be a capability loss without a clinical or safety incident.

Reference traces should exercise at least a timely route, a permitted alternative, safe late prevention, late complete handoff, declined external offer, partial restoration, missing acknowledgement and missing evaluation evidence. Completion must not lose credit because it no longer resembles an incomplete partial anchor. Route applicability follows authorised choices, not later success. Status questions take their correct answers from the actual state at the time, including unexpectedly successful earlier work.

Avoid turning the new slate into six revision-and-cancellation puzzles. Its primary mechanisms should remain distinct: W01 authority propagation; E01 support commitments; C01 end-to-end dependencies; H01 constrained diversion and reconciliation; W02 custody and recall; G01 suitable placement and onward responsibility. Repeated mechanisms remain correlated observations. A broader sector claim needs independently authored cases, not just more repeats of one week.

Keep realistic safeguards in the proposed baseline. If a tool normally blocks an invalid release or duplicate stock movement, a blocked attempt is not an executed failure. Any deliberately weakened guard configuration must be separately identified; otherwise the benchmark could attribute the harness's unusual affordances to ordinary deployment conditions. No source reviewed establishes how often the proposed mistakes occur or how likely a model is to make them.

The proposed 75-minute weekly owner-review budget is still a study design to measure, not evidence that supervision is sufficient. Required clinical, scientific and operational approvals are additional human work. Reviewing a completed episode measures detection; prevention requires an intervention before the relevant event. If real reviewers are absent, the resulting report can discuss completion and reporting fidelity but not measured supervision savings.

## Archive and next decisions

The [archive](../scenario-archive/README.md) now holds eleven commercial weeks and three adjacent weeks: steel quality, referral administration and Universal Credit reconsideration. Their recent fixes remain preserved, as does the government brief. The earlier broad-scope research is retained as history. This is a change to the active comparison, not a judgement that those jobs are unimportant.

Water reporting is the only previously authored full week kept active. New electricity, government-welfare, pathology, telecom and alternative-water drafts bring the active authored count to six. Recycling relevant inventory, reservation or version-history mechanics is sensible, but carrying across commercial authority and deadlines unchanged is not. The current [scope](critical-infrastructure-scope.md) and [index](README.md) replace the former healthcare-first/steel-second sequence.

The six drafts now have a [joint authoring review](draft-suite-review.md); obtain practitioner feedback on their realism and distinct decisions before product integration. For a later first operator pilot, secure water-reporting and distribution-operator continuity practitioners; populate non-sensitive source packs; implement the state transitions through the existing engine; and measure effort, coverage and cost. Only after those checks can a separately priced pilot establish whether the cases discriminate models or expose unclear tools and rules. A strong positive result remains publishable.

Evidence reviewed through 14 September 2026 supports the workflow choices and their boundaries. It does not establish protected asset designation, complete current legal compliance, practitioner validation, executable simulator behaviour, empirical difficulty, customer demand or real-world safety. Exact private SOPs, contractual data-sharing arrangements and technical acceptance procedures remain the main evidence gaps. These are targeted next inputs, not reasons to fill the gaps with invented national rules.
