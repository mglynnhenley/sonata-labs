# Review of the six continuity scenario drafts

Authoring review, 14 September 2026. Read alongside the [catalogue](README.md), [research](critical-infrastructure-research.md) and [rubric method](rubric-method.md). These packs are intended for later product integration. Specialist-agent reviews and document checks inform this review; no practitioner walkthrough, executed reference week or model comparison has been completed.

| [W02 alternative water](alternative-water-supply.md) | Public-supplier logistics desk: batch-accounted final deliveries, recall containment and replacement supply | Conserve physical quantities through depot, vehicle, distribution point, recipient and return states | Assistance logistics plus evidence propagation and custody |

Retain all six for the draft review. W01 and W02 share a supplier setting but own different endpoints: reporting versus physical distribution and recall. G01 contributes household choice and suitability, but should remain visibly separate in an operator-only result. C01 does not establish real emergency-call completion or emergency-response capacity. None delegates clinical, scientific, engineering or emergency-command judgement to the agent.

The strongest common task is maintaining responsibility as evidence and commitments change. Six sector or desk labels do not create six independent measurements. In particular, E01, G01 and W02 form a logistics cluster; E01, H01 and C01 share scoped recovery; nearly every case depends on acceptance, actual execution, evidence, communication and handoff. Later reporting should expose these shared mechanisms alongside case results.

## Corrections made during authoring review

The packs distinguish planned work from accepted commitments and actual receipts. They preserve valid alternative allocations, early prevention and late completion; later useful work cannot reduce already earned partial credit. Authority stays with named qualified counterparts, and the agent must describe residual gaps even when technical recovery remains unavailable.

The research summaries now match the authored events: H01 restores a read-only comparison view; C01's Wednesday problem is expiring accepted site support, not an observed power failure; G01 has two baseline accessible providers without guaranteed additional rescue capacity after a missed window. W01's initial updates and revision corrections explicitly average recipient units, including mixed on-time and late receipt outcomes.

W02 now follows every actual recalled recipient, including households served early under valid authority. An unaffected H25 route keeps its original deadline; only a recall-affected undelivered route receives the qualified extension. Friday quantities, bounded manifest reservations, late vehicle-return slots and the larger recall cohort's contact and transport capacity are explicit. Its reference ledger conserves 62 cases, including 16 returned B17 cases and four cases reported consumed before recall.

Recovery is descriptive across the slate: detection, repair, notification, elapsed exposure and residual work. W01 retains case-specific numeric anchors, which must not be pooled with other packs' unscored recovery. Incident severity and reporting anchors also differ in authoring depth. Preserve the omitted or misstated fact, affected recipients and correction history; raw severity counts or reporting percentages are not calibrated equivalents.

| C01 | Two accepted site alternatives and a 17-call response window across five opportunities | Complete counted week and execution |
| W02 | Conserved 62-case ledger; 19-call reference and 38-call larger-cohort windows across four and seven opportunities | Complete counted week and execution |

These are calculations under proposed tool contracts. They demonstrate particular feasible routes, not realistic human workload or model success. One signed report may contain several bounded test cases, while other tools expose individual movements or messages; call totals partly reflect interface granularity. Whole-week slack and a successful local window are different kinds of evidence.

The shared fifteen-minute clock, predictable counterpart replies and Friday-to-Monday handoff pattern help control comparisons. They also create a routine an agent could learn across cases. The current drafts do not sample unreliable communications, ordinary variable response times or urgent off-hours command work. Practitioner review should determine which controls remain credible in each desk.

## Three questions for the next review

1. **Does each desk have credible authority and support?** Ask relevant practitioners to inspect delegated decisions, qualified instructions, service boundaries and counterpart availability. Public guidance grounds the setting; it does not supply every local procedure or validate invented deadlines and quantities. W01 additionally needs the applicable notification guidance before regulatory implementation.
2. **Does the whole week feel like the work being claimed?** Walk through opening records without the answer key, including authorised alternatives and missed windows. Measure active effort, waiting and ambiguity. Review event density and the repeated quarter-hour response pattern before treating these as comparable five-day workloads.
3. **Which scores can support a comparison?** Freeze unit denominators, incident opportunities, reporting anchors and recovery attribution. Equal weighting of five utility families is an authoring convention. Calibrate on complete, blinded traces before claiming that equivalent points across desks represent equivalent work or risk.

## Later integration handoff

After the draft and practitioner review, W01 and E01 remain the suggested first operator cases to integrate. Populate versioned source records and agent-visible briefs; translate the proposed transitions through the existing engine; retain all operational state in resettable snapshots. Counterpart and injected events must remain separate from agent actions.

Execute successful, late, preventive and authorised-alternative routes, checking actual artifacts and receipts through the full horizon. Report missing evidence and incomplete execution as evaluation limitations. Measure call volume, human effort and costs before choosing a separately priced pilot. These documents author neither a second runner nor permission for a paid benchmark matrix.
