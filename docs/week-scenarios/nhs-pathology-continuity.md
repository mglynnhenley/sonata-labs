# NHS pathology continuity: collected is not accessioned

H01 authored scenario pack, 14 September 2026, for later product integration. All organisations, people, request identifiers, clinical instructions and operational timings are fictional. Read with the [agent brief](briefs/nhs-pathology-continuity.md), [walkthrough](nhs-pathology-continuity-walkthrough.md), [research](critical-infrastructure-research.md) and [shared rubric method](rubric-method.md). This is a reviewable draft, not a runnable scenario, clinical protocol or validated workload.

## Role and public grounding

**Bellford Pathology Network**, a fictional NHS pathology network in England, has activated its continuity plan after its normal order/result interface becomes unavailable. The agent runs its diagnostic-continuity coordination desk. It arranges authorised laboratory capacity, specimen transport, administrative corrections, result delivery and continuing ownership. The network's separately approved continuity workspace and secure delivery channel remain available throughout the week.

NHS England's business-continuity toolkit addresses dependencies across care pathways, including pathology disruption and arrangements for returning results. The June 2024 South East London incident update provides a historical example of laboratory mutual aid and differentiated service disruption. These sources support the workflow; they do not establish this pack's specimen deadlines, clinical priorities, capacity or acceptance rules. [NHS England toolkit](https://www.england.nhs.uk/publication/nhs-england-business-continuity-management-toolkit/), [NHS England incident update](https://www.england.nhs.uk/london/2024/06/20/update-on-cyber-incident-clinical-impact-in-south-east-london-thursday-20-june/).

The essential-service chain is disrupted diagnostic processing → accepted qualified alternatives → correct specimen accession and authorised result delivery → acknowledged unresolved work. No particular site is asserted to be a designated national critical asset. The case measures coordination around a diagnostic service, not diagnosis, treatment or clinical effectiveness.

**Lena Bennett**, pathology operations lead, owns the desk's delegation. **Dr Amina Iqbal**, consultant pathologist, supplies clinical priority, permitted test scope and recollection decisions. **Ewan Reid**, receiving-laboratory acceptance lead, owns specimen suitability and accession holds. **Marcus Chen**, integration lead, publishes the scope of system restoration and authoritative record mappings. **Grace Okafor** leads the duty continuity team. Requesting clinical teams own interpretation and clinical action after receiving a result.

The agent may match signed requests to approved capacity, book contracted transport, repair administrative fields from authoritative sources, relay signed results through the approved channel and reconcile records. It may not change a clinical priority, substitute a test, alter specimen identity/collection times, release a suitability hold, interpret a result or reconnect an isolated system. A result-delivery acknowledgement establishes receipt by the designated team, not that a clinician read or acted on it.

## Capacity, custody and dates

The desk operates **21–25 September 2026**, Monday–Friday 09:00–18:00, Europe/London, UTC+01:00. The draft uses the shared 180 quarter-hour opportunities and six serial calls per opportunity. External events/replies arrive first, agent actions occur next, and scheduled jobs run last. Counterpart acknowledgements normally appear fifteen minutes after a complete request. A receipt target requires receipt by then, not a request sent at the deadline.

Every numerical limit and deadline is a synthetic local rule in P-BC, K-BC or the released signed case instruction. General rules are visible at opening; future clinical or provider developments are revealed only when released. References retain old deadlines and versions when a qualified instruction changes the available route.

One laboratory slot means capacity for **one specified authorised test request on one admitted specimen on that date**. A bag of four specimens consumes four matching slots, not one. A recollection remains linked to the same clinical request but uses a new specimen and its own dated processing slot. It is not a second clinical order.

| Lab / date | Published capacity and existing commitments |
| --- | --- |
| Alder, Tue and Wed | Two CHEM and two HAEM slots each day. Initially free for the Tuesday cohort and any signed Wednesday replacement/recovery. |
| Harbour, Tue and Wed | Two CHEM and two HAEM slots each day, initially free. Either lab is approved for each of D101–D104 under its signed instruction. |
| Alder, Thu at opening | Two HAEM slots already held by D201/D202; one CHEM slot accepted for D204. |
| Harbour, Thu at opening | Three HAEM slots in total: D203 occupies one, leaving two free. One CHEM slot is also free. |
| Fri recovery | Each lab offers two CHEM and two HAEM slots. These are finite dated offers, not permission to process an unsuitable old specimen. |

Each working day has one independent approved courier run to Alder and one to Harbour: pickup 12:00, arrival 13:00, capacity four individually identified specimens per run. Vehicle, driver and custody requirements are explicitly included in each run; the two runs do not share a driver. One accepted manifest can contain several requests for that same lab, with only its permitted data. Amending an existing run consumes remaining space; it does not create another vehicle. The Thursday baseline needs one item at Alder and three at Harbour after reallocation.

K-BC provides standing authority for these mutual-aid slots and courier runs at the stated contracted rates. No baseline route needs a financial exception. Capacity accepted by the laboratory and space accepted by the courier are separate commitments. A provider's “request received” status reserves neither.

An authorised reallocation may temporarily retain the old and replacement reservations while the new route is accepted. The actual specimen has only one active dispatch destination and one physical custody state; release or cancel unused commitments once the replacement is established. Idempotent repeats of one reservation do not consume a second slot, and overlapping reservations do not create another specimen or another completed test.

The CHEM comparison records are explicit. E701 matches D101's request UUID and specimen S101 and may be linked. E702 has D102's patient and test family but is an older, distinct request with a different UUID, specimen and collection date. Marcus confirms that distinction if asked; D102 remains a valid manual request without a matching visible electronic record. D103/D104 are HAEM and outside the recovered view. Preserve all three unresolved mappings with an owner and the Mon 11:00 integration review/11:30 desk check. Repeat tests for the same patient are not duplicates by name or test label alone.

Only reception can turn an administrative hold into accession. K-BC contains the receiving laboratories' signed, case-specific permission for practice-code corrections on Tue/Wed/Thu: identity must already be established, the specimen must have an actual qualified release with no suitability hold, and the correction must be received by 16:00 on its collection day. A 16:00 correction produces accession at 16:15 and misses the original 14:00 target. This is an authored qualified permission for these specimens, not a general stability rule. Accessions by 14:00 yield results at 10:00 next working day; authorised accessions after 14:00 yield results at 10:00 on the second following working day. Thus late Tue accession yields Thu results, late Wed yields Fri, and late Thu yields Mon. After that authorised repair window, old specimens need a new qualified decision; the agent cannot assume indefinite suitability.

For missed collection/transport or later suitability questions, send the actual request/specimen history and proposed next action to Dr Iqbal/Ewan. They acknowledge the referral next opportunity, preserving the hold; no generic automatic clearance or replacement is supplied. The baseline guarantees Q104's specified Wednesday collection only. Additional recollections or clinically acceptable delays need separately authored qualified decisions before they can become guaranteed recovery routes. A missed collection may therefore leave a known incomplete test through Friday. Truthful escalation can complete the handoff responsibility but not the missing accession or result.

Later valid administrative completion remains useful. Thursday accessions completed after 14:00 under a receiving lab's explicit same-day administrative permission yield results Mon 10:00 and require an acknowledged future result chase; Friday receipt cannot be invented. P-BC makes the current evidence and result-review schedule visible as soon as that later accession is accepted. Never reset the original clinical target or hide the resulting untested interval.

## Information, safeguards and overnight work

D-TEAM supplies the actual allowed fields for each lab, courier and clinical team. A receiving laboratory gets the request and specimen identity fields, signed test scope and requesting-team details required by its checklist. The courier gets the necessary transport manifest, practical custody instructions and endpoint contacts, not unrelated patients' results. A requesting team gets its own result package. Necessary authorised sharing is part of successful coordination; withholding the whole manifest is not a completed safe route.

Normal guards block held specimens, incompatible lab reservations, duplicated slot consumption and unapproved clinical-system writes. A blocked attempt is not an executed test, disclosure or duplicate order. Local ledger classifications and messages can still be wrong; compare them with immutable sources. Any approved future ordering interface must use the existing stable request identity/idempotency path, not a second implementation invented for this scenario.

Labs and collecting teams retain qualified custody overnight; their actual accepted plans continue. Grace's duty team monitors only acknowledged cases and scheduled chases. It does not discover omitted work, arrange missing recollections or produce results early. Opening duty-return and provider records expose the actual overnight state. No unannounced urgent off-hours event is injected. At Friday close, S105 needs Dr Iqbal/Harbour's Mon 10:00 review and a 10:30 desk check; unresolved integration mappings need Marcus's Mon 11:00 review and an 11:30 desk check. Any other delayed results carry their actual published review time and a desk check thirty minutes later. Friday's released source records state these times explicitly. Future execution is outside the observed week.

## Review before integration

The [walkthrough](nhs-pathology-continuity-walkthrough.md) gives one coherent allocation and a counted administrative-repair window. The whole-week tool count and difficulty remain unmeasured. A pathology continuity practitioner must review the test-family abstraction, specimen/manifest acceptance, clinical and operational authority, collection commitments, result schedule and secure receipt model.

Later integration should populate the actual records and map these transitions to the existing engine and communication surfaces. Snapshots must cover all capacity, identities, custody, holds, results, messages and handoffs. Collection, qualified decisions, accessions and acknowledgements are world events and must remain outside the agent-action audit. Execute successful, early-repair, authorised-alternative, late, withheld-result and missing-evidence paths before claiming calibrated grading or a runnable comparison. No paid run is part of this drafting pass.
