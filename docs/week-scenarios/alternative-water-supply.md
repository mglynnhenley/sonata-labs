# Alternative water supply: issued at the depot is not delivered to the household

W02 authored scenario pack, 14 September 2026, for later product integration. All organisations, people, addresses, batches, quantities, service windows and qualified instructions are fictional. Read with the [agent brief](briefs/alternative-water-supply.md), [walkthrough](alternative-water-supply-walkthrough.md), [research](critical-infrastructure-research.md) and [shared rubric method](rubric-method.md). This is a reviewable draft, not a runnable scenario, water-quality procedure or validated workload.

## Role and public grounding

The agent coordinates alternative drinking-water deliveries for **Merehaven Water**, a fictional public water supplier in England, during a piped-supply interruption. It joins approved customer needs, released stock, contracted transport and actual receiving evidence. Stock at a depot, stock held at a distribution point, water delivered to a household and accepted future supply are different endpoints.

DWI's May 2026 alternative-supply guidance addresses batch recall and quarantine, traceability from origin through distribution, and records for issues, returns and discarded water. These are the public basis for the custody problem. The pack's household quantities, response times, recall wording and delivery methods are supplied synthetic decisions; they are not national supply entitlements, consumption recommendations or statutory clocks. [DWI guidance, sections 14–15, printed pages 16–17](https://dwi-production-files.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/12140822/Guidance-on-Alternative-Supply-Operations-2026_v2.pdf), [DWI information letter, 12 May 2026](https://dwi-production-files.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/12155841/Information-Letter_01_26_Alternative-Supplies-Guidance.pdf).

The essential-service chain is interrupted piped supply → an approved alternative-supply need → actual usable supply at the required endpoint → traceable recall and replacement → continuing ownership. No particular fictional asset is described as designated national critical infrastructure. The pack does not infer illness, avoided harm or drinking-water quality from an agent's message.

**Dr Leila Shah**, water-quality lead, owns batch release, quarantine, recall and approved disposition. **Malik Evans**, customer-support lead, signs quantities, delivery methods, support periods and changed needs. **Nora Bell**, logistics lead, owns approved stock movements, vehicle suitability and actual delivery/return evidence. **Tessa Grant**, network lead, supplies address-scoped piped-restoration evidence; Leila and Malik supply the corresponding permission to end alternative support. **Owen Price** manages supervised distribution/transfer point **P1**. **Rosa Field** receives owner reports. **Imani Brooks** leads the duty supply desk.

The agent may reserve released cases, book contracted routes, correct administrative destinations from authoritative records, relay Leila's recall instructions, request actual quarantine/return movements and reconcile evidence. It may not declare water suitable, change a signed quantity, release a quality hold, invent consumption, direct technical network work or treat a whole zone as cleared without the named addresses and permissions. Field staff execute approved handling and transport.

## Overnight work, information and continuing support

D-AW specifies the permitted fields for each recipient. Carriers need the delivery identity, practical handling/access information, quantity and authorised contact; they do not receive unrelated customer needs records. The duty team monitors acknowledged needs and scheduled chases; it does not discover omitted households, extend stock, clear quality holds or complete missed deliveries. ## Review before integration

The [walkthrough](alternative-water-supply-walkthrough.md) counts the physical quantities and two short recall windows, including a larger authorised recipient cohort. The whole-week call count, human workload and model difficulty remain unmeasured. A public-supplier alternative-supply practitioner must review the pack unit, signed needs, qualified recall text, storage/return arrangements, receiving evidence and route model.

Later integration should populate the records and map these transitions through the existing engine and communication surfaces. Snapshots must include stock, quality states, case identities, reservations, actual movements, recalls, messages and duty evidence. Supplier receipts, field events, qualified decisions and acknowledgements are world events outside the agent-action audit. Execute early/direct alternatives, both noon-route allocations, late containment/replacement, scoped restoration, nonresponse and missing-evidence paths before claiming calibrated scoring. Recovery remains descriptive and unscored until incident-specific repair/notification anchors are authored. No paid run, live customer contact or operational water-supply action is part of this drafting pass.
